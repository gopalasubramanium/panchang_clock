Make it yours

Times follow the selected location, including daylight saving.

Search stays on your device. Results use city-centre coordinates and time zones.

Traditional names support 12 languages. New explanatory text is currently in English. Tamil solar month names are shown as a reference; a complete regional solar calendar is still under review.

Your Panchang stays with you

Calculations run on your device. There are no advertising, analytics, account, or remote calculation services. Your settings and personal dates are stored locally.

Location access is optional and requested only when you choose it. Your browser or operating system may use its own location service. Sharing passes your chosen content to the service you select. A web host may log normal page requests.

Use “Delete local data” to remove settings and personal dates on this device. Calendar entries already exported to another app are managed there.
