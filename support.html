A little help with your day.

Find your location

Open Settings and search for a city. The bundled list covers 34,145 city centres. Check the time zone and elevation, then save. For a smaller place, use a nearby city or manual coordinates. Searches stay on your device.

Why does another calendar show a different answer?

Compare the same date, local time, coordinates, time zone, sunrise convention, ayanamsa and lunar month convention. Amanta and Purnimanta month names can differ legitimately. “Until” means the current value ends at that time. A Panchang day usually follows sunrise, rather than midnight.

Festival dates are clearly labelled previews. Regional precedence, Smarta/Vaishnava fasting and Parana rules are not complete. Do not use preview markers as a certified fasting schedule.

Calendar exports and reminders

In Calendar, select categories and a reminder lead time, then Export this month. The calendar file is free and can be imported into your calendar app. Imported markers represent the disclosed deciding time or window; a one-minute marker is not a ceremony duration. Calendar apps and device settings determine whether reminders appear. The web app does not promise background notifications.

Install and use offline

On iPhone or iPad: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android or desktop: use your browser’s install option. Wait for “Offline ready” before disconnecting. Native downloads and their signing status are listed in releases. A simulator build is not an iPhone installer.

Report a problem

Use “Report a difference” inside the app to prepare a draft with calculation settings. Review it, add a reference and describe what happened. Nothing is sent automatically. Submit non-sensitive reports through GitHub support.
