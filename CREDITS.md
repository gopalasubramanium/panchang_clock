# Why I made Eksaar Panchang

By [Gopala Subramanium](https://me.sgopala.com).

A basic daily reference should be easy to use, freely available, and free of advertising, paywalls and unnecessary clutter. That is why I made Eksaar Panchang: to make a small part of everyday life simpler, without asking for an account or interrupting it with promotions.

Other developers may reasonably choose different funding models. This is the approach I want for this app: useful, respectful, open and free to use.

Astronomy Engine and the other open-source dependencies make this possible. Their authors and licenses are acknowledged in `public/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`. Calculation conventions, measured accuracy and remaining traditional-rule limitations are documented in the app and `docs/ACCURACY.md`.
