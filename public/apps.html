Android, Linux and the web

The web app works on Android, Linux and other modern devices. Use your browser’s install option and wait for “Offline ready” before disconnecting.

Android is in closed testing, with a native update in development. A public Google Play release is not available yet. Linux AppImage and Debian packages are available as beta downloads; these packages are unsigned.

Store availability depends on your country and device. Apple distribution in France is pending clearance.
