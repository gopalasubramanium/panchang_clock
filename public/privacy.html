Your Panchang stays with you.

Updated 25 September 2026 · Includes the native iPhone and iPad app

Eksaar Panchang is made by Gopala Subramanium as a free daily reference. There are no ads, analytics SDKs, accounts, subscriptions, or remote calculation services in this app.

What stays on your device

Calculations, selected coordinates, calendar preferences, saved places and personal lunar dates are processed locally. The native iPhone and iPad app saves this information in protected app files using Apple’s operating-system file protection and excludes it from automatic cloud backup. The web and other desktop/mobile versions use browser or app storage without additional app-level encryption. Someone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to read stored information. Use your device’s passcode and disk protection.

The web app caches its own files and city list for offline use. Storage can be cleared by your browser or operating system. Keep a backup of dates you want to retain.

Location and reminders are optional

Device location is requested only when you explicitly choose the current-location control. It is not tracked in the background. Your browser or operating system controls its location service. You can instead search the bundled city list or enter coordinates. Native notification permission is requested only when you ask for a reminder; delivery depends on device settings. The iPhone and iPad app opens Apple’s calendar editor for you to review and save an event. It does not request access to read your calendar database. Your chosen calendar account controls how saved events sync.

When information leaves your device

  • Loading or updating the website makes ordinary requests to its host. Cloudflare serves the custom domain; GitHub Pages serves the alternate website. These providers can receive IP addresses, request metadata and URLs under their own policies. The app does not add analytics.
  • City search queries are processed locally. The city dataset is downloaded as a bundled file, without your search text.
  • Sharing is initiated by you. A summary includes the selected place name and date. A calculation link additionally includes coordinates and settings in its URL fragment; recipients can read those details. Fragments are not included in HTTP requests. Older query-based links can expose their parameters to hosting logs.
  • Exports and backups can include personal names, location and calculation data. Exported files and reminders are controlled by the receiving app. The report feature prepares a local draft and sends nothing automatically.
  • External links open sites with their own privacy practices. Support issues on GitHub are public; do not post sensitive information there.

Deleting and backing up

On iPhone and iPad, delete individual entries in My dates and cancel pending reminders in Settings. Use Settings → Export a backup before removing the app if you want to keep your dates. Deleting the app (rather than offloading it) removes its local data. On the web and other app versions, Privacy → Delete local data removes preferences, places, personal dates, temporary native exports and pending app reminders on the current device; clearing browser site data also removes cached web files. Android backup exclusions are configured for app data. These actions do not remove backups you exported or events you saved in a calendar; remove those separately in their destination apps.

Questions or security concerns

Use support issues for non-sensitive app questions. For a vulnerability, use the repository’s private vulnerability report. Do not include passwords, personal dates or precise location unless necessary and deliberately chosen.
